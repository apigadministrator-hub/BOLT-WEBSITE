
# APIG-37 — APIG and APIG Monster News Media Identity and Separation Specification

## Purpose

APIG and APIG Monster News Media are distinct identities operating within the broader APIG website and infrastructure.

This specification establishes the separation between the two identities, including their purposes, branding, logos, website presentation, navigation, content functions, and relationship to external news-media organizations.

The two identities shall remain clearly distinguishable while operating within a unified technical platform.

## APIG Identity

The primary APIG identity represents the professional investigative and public-information functions of the organization.

APIG includes, but is not limited to:

* Government and jurisdictional information
* People and public officials
* Positions and offices
* Agencies and organizations
* Government relationships and authority
* Meetings
* Documents and records
* Sources and provenance
* Evidence and verification
* Investigative research
* Structured public information
* Search and navigation of the APIG information system

The APIG identity shall use the official **APIG medallion-style logo**.

The APIG identity represents the professional, investigative, informational, and system-oriented functions of the website.

## APIG Monster News Media Identity

**APIG Monster News Media** is the distinct news-media identity within the APIG website.

Its purpose is to provide a news-media presentation and editorial function separate from the primary APIG investigative and information-system presentation.

APIG Monster News Media shall use the official **APIG Monster News Media logo**.

The APIG Monster News Media identity shall not be presented as interchangeable with the primary APIG identity.

## Logo Separation

The APIG resource system shall maintain the two logos as separate assets:

* `APIG logo.png`
* `APIG Monster News Media logo.png`

Each logo shall be used only in the context for which it is designated.

The APIG logo shall be used for the primary APIG website and its professional investigative, informational, search, and navigation functions.

The APIG Monster News Media logo shall be used for the News Media section and its associated editorial and news-media presentation.

Neither logo shall be substituted for the other merely for visual convenience.

## Website Separation

The APIG website may operate as a unified technical system while presenting distinct functional areas.

The primary APIG interface shall maintain its professional investigative and information-system identity.

The APIG Monster News Media section shall have a recognizable news-media identity, including its designated logo and appropriate editorial presentation.

Users shall be able to understand when they are viewing:

1. The primary APIG investigative and information system; or
2. The APIG Monster News Media section.

The separation shall be achieved through branding, navigation, page presentation, and content organization rather than requiring separate technical websites unless future requirements determine otherwise.

## APIG Monster News Media Structure

The News Media section shall provide an expandable structure for APIG Monster's own news-media content and for coverage of relevant external news organizations.

The initial structure should include:

### Featured News

A prominent top-level **featured news or news flashpoint** area shall highlight the most important current APIG Monster News Media story or stories.

This area shall provide a clear entry point to the most significant current news coverage.

### APIG Monster News

Below the featured area, APIG Monster's original news-media articles shall be presented in an organized and searchable manner.

Articles may be organized by:

* Date
* Subject
* Jurisdiction
* Topic
* Person
* Organization
* Event
* Other appropriate categories

The structure shall remain expandable as the volume of APIG Monster reporting increases.

## External and Affiliated News Media

The APIG Monster News Media section may maintain dedicated sections for relevant external news organizations and independent publishers.

Examples may include organizations such as:

* Village Voice
* Edgar County Watchdogs

These examples are not intended to limit the system.

The architecture shall allow additional legitimate news-media organizations to be added without redesigning the News Media section.

## External Article References

When APIG Monster identifies a relevant article published by an external news organization, APIG may provide a concise summary or description explaining the subject and significance of the external article.

The purpose of the summary is to help APIG visitors understand why the article is relevant.

APIG shall direct users to the original publisher for the complete article.

APIG shall not unnecessarily reproduce or rewrite the complete external article.

The original publisher shall remain identifiable, and the external article shall retain its relationship to the original source.

## Referral Function

External-news references may serve as a referral mechanism that directs APIG visitors to the original publisher.

The system should make the original source and destination clear and accessible.

The purpose is to increase discoverability of relevant journalism while providing APIG users with useful contextual information.

## Editorial Independence

External news organizations referenced by APIG Monster News Media shall remain distinct from APIG.

An external organization shall not be presented as an APIG subsidiary, department, partner, employee, or representative unless an actual documented relationship exists.

References to external organizations shall accurately identify the originating publisher.

## Expansion

The News Media architecture shall be designed to accommodate additional external news organizations, independent publishers, and other relevant media sources.

Adding a new source should require the creation or configuration of the appropriate source record and presentation components rather than a fundamental redesign of the website.

Each external media source should be treated as an identifiable entity or asset where appropriate under the APIG information and asset-management architecture.

## Relationship to APIG Information Architecture

News-media content shall remain connected to the broader APIG information system where appropriate.

An article may be associated with:

* People
* Agencies
* Organizations
* Positions
* Locations
* Meetings
* Documents
* Events
* Sources
* Investigations
* Other APIG entities

These relationships allow News Media content to participate in the broader APIG information architecture without eliminating the distinction between the APIG investigative identity and the APIG Monster News Media identity.

## Navigation

The primary APIG navigation shall clearly identify the News Media section as a distinct area.

Navigation into APIG Monster News Media should transition the presentation into the APIG Monster identity while preserving access to the broader APIG information system.

Users should be able to navigate between APIG investigative information and APIG Monster News Media without confusing the two functions.

## Governance

The distinction between APIG and APIG Monster News Media shall be treated as a governed architectural and branding requirement.

Future applications, pages, content systems, and interfaces shall preserve this distinction unless an intentional change to the organizational architecture is formally established.

Changes to either identity's logo, purpose, navigation, editorial structure, or relationship to the other identity shall be documented and reconciled with this specification and all affected APIG specifications.
